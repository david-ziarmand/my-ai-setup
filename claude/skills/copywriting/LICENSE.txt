Changes made

1. Skill frontmatter removed, all files moved to one flat references/ folder, links fixed.
2. Em dashes and en dashes replaced with colons, commas, or hyphens, except where a dash is the example of the pattern
   to avoid or the character to search for (ai-tells.md, humanizer.md).
3. Dash policy made strict: the "1-2 per page in long copy" allowance (copy-editing.md, checklist.md, ai-tells.md) and
   the "match the writer's sample" exception (humanizer.md) removed. No em or en dashes anywhere.
4. The product marketing context step in copywriting.md and copy-editing.md now points to the project AGENTS.md,
   docs/, and the brief.
5. References to skills that are not part of this skill removed (cro, emails, popups, offers, ab-testing,
   marketing-psychology, ai-seo). "Related Skills" links point to files in this folder or to the seo skill.
